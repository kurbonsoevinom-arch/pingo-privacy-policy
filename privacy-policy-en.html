About PINGO

PINGO is a language-learning app provided by LEADER LANGUAGES.

Information that may be processed

  • Name and email address.
  • Authentication information managed by the authentication provider; raw passwords should not be stored by the app.
  • Learning activity and progress, including vocabulary progress, XP, goals, and achievements where enabled.
  • Messages sent to the AI tutor and its responses.
  • Speech input may be converted to text by device or operating-system speech recognition. Text submitted to the tutor may then be sent to the AI service.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app requests microphone access for speaking and pronunciation practice. Speech recognition may be provided by the operating system or its service providers. Depending on device and settings, audio may be processed on-device or by a provider. Do not speak sensitive personal information into the app.

AI tutor

Messages and relevant recent conversation context may be sent through a Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI to generate a response. Project code references the OpenAI API and gpt-4o-mini. Verify production configuration and provider retention terms before publishing. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Service providers and use

The project is configured for Supabase backend functionality and authentication. Providers may process information needed to operate enabled features. Information may be used to secure accounts, provide lessons, save/sync progress, answer AI requests, troubleshoot, and protect the app. Confirm actual production storage and all analytics, advertising, and crash-reporting SDKs before publishing.
