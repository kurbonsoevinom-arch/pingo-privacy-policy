PINGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. About PINGO

PINGO is a language-learning application provided by LEADER LANGUAGES.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, PINGO may process your name, email address, account information, learning progress, vocabulary results, XP, goals, achievements, and messages sent to the AI tutor.

Authentication information is managed by the authentication provider. The application should not store your raw account password directly.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

PINGO may request microphone access for speaking and pronunciation practice. Speech recognition may be provided by your device, operating system, or a service provider. Depending on your device and settings, audio may be processed on your device or by a provider. You can manage microphone permissions in your device settings.

4. AI Tutor

When you use the AI tutor, your message and relevant conversation context may be sent through a secure backend function to OpenAI to generate a response. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive personal information to the AI tutor.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to authenticate users, provide lessons, save and synchronize learning progress, operate AI features, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve the application.

6. Service Providers and Sharing

PINGO may use Supabase for backend and authentication functions and OpenAI for AI-generated responses. These providers may process information necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Users may contact us to request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

8. Children's Privacy

Parents or guardians should review the application and its features before allowing a child to use it. The service must comply with applicable children's privacy laws and platform requirements. Contact us with questions about a child's information.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Secret API keys must be kept on secure servers and must not be embedded in the mobile application.

10. Your Choices

You can manage microphone permissions through your device settings and contact us regarding access to or deletion of your personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.
